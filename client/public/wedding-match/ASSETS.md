## hero-romantic-couple.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a real raster photograph for the right-hand portrait hero of a warm romantic Vietnamese wedding marketplace; this is a standalone photo, never a website screenshot. Generate a NEW fictional young adult Asian bride and groom viewed mostly from behind, standing together by a serene misty lake with softly layered green-grey mountains in the distance. Bride has a flowing ivory-white bridal gown with delicate sheer fabric texture and a long translucent veil, groom wears an elegant ivory-beige suit. Their natural hands are gently joined, heads turned slightly toward each other but faces mostly unseen; believable anatomy, no recognizable real identities. A graceful floral wedding arch with plentiful white, cream and soft blush-pink roses frames the right and upper edges, with a few subtle sage leaves and a glimpse of a garden wedding aisle. Composition: PORTRAIT 3:4 image, couple near center-lower area, full gown and veil visible, mountains/lake in upper-middle distance, generous calm airy space at top-left, flowers gracefully framing rather than obscuring the couple. Lighting: soft golden morning sunlight through light haze, romantic premium editorial wedding photography, realistic lens depth, natural textile and flower detail. Palette: warm ivory #FCF6EA, champagne #F1DBA2, pale peach, blush coral #E38C82, muted sage #98AB98; rich soft shadows, restrained saturation, no harsh black. Image must feel intimate, luminous and refined. No text, calligraphy, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, UI, button, border, mascot, cartoon, collage or visible photographer. No extra guests. Opaque photo background.

## categories-editorial-grid.png

Use case: product-mockup, premium wedding editorial photography. Asset type: ONE raster contact sheet of eight wedding-category thumbnail photographs, for CSS cropping into separate website cards. Canvas MUST be landscape aspect ratio 2:1, arranged as exactly FOUR EQUAL COLUMNS by TWO EQUAL ROWS; all eight cells square, edge-to-edge, NO gutters, NO visible grid lines, NO borders, NO labels or text anywhere. Cell boundaries exactly x=25%,50%,75% and y=50%. Keep each category subject entirely inside its own cell with 10% breathing room. No object may cross a cell boundary. Consistent warm ivory/cream, pale champagne, blush peach-pink and muted sage color story, photorealistic materials, soft natural window light, elegant airy editorial quality. Eight cells in this exact reading order: TOP ROW LEFT TO RIGHT: (1) a classic silver-and-black camera beside a small ivory floral wedding photograph, no visible brand or lettering; (2) a neatly wrapped small wedding gift with pale blush ribbon and elegant blank cream invitation stationery, no lettering; (3) an intimate celebration still life of two champagne flutes on a softly decorated wedding table with pale roses and ivory napkins, no people; (4) an open wedding-planning notebook and a blank paper calendar with fine empty square grid, a pencil and a tiny blush flower, absolutely no words or digits. BOTTOM ROW LEFT TO RIGHT: (5) soft makeup brushes with pale blush handles beside a neutral cosmetic compact and pearl detail, no labels; (6) a beautiful freestanding white-and-blush floral wedding arch in a warm garden, centered and fully visible, no people; (7) an elegant outdoor garden wedding banquet venue with cream round tables, soft ivory chairs and modest blush flowers, no people; (8) an ivory wedding dress next to an elegant beige groom's suit vest/jacket on simple display stands, no heads or bodies and no lettering. Make the arrangement unmistakably an exact 4-by-2 contact sheet of independent photographic category pictures, consistent refined mood, uncluttered compositions and clear subject silhouettes at small thumbnail scale. Not a website/UI mockup. No text, title, captions, numbers, typography, logo, watermark, illustration, mascot, buttons or rounded-card outlines. Opaque backgrounds.
